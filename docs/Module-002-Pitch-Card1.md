# IT265 Module 2: Concept Pitch Cards

Copy this card for each of your two or three shortlisted concepts. Aim to explain each in about one minute. Use your [idea bank](./01-idea-bank.md) as a starting point.

## Pitch Card

**Working title:**
- Tread

**Player role and situation:**
- Players control a tank that roams a battlefield covered in a fog of war to look for pieces of a map to a goal. There are 2 different roles, with one player taking the role as the Tank's "Driver", and the other as the "Gunner". The tank they control is a **Casemate** tank, which has a fixed turret, requiring teamwork to use effectively.

**Repeated decision or action:**
- Each turn, the players, depending on their role, makes a decision from a list of actions.
- Drivers are responsible for controlling movement and assisting the gunner, as the casemate tank can only attack in front of itself.
- Gunners are responsible for controlling attack actions, both with the main gun and a personal weapon for covering areas not in front of the tank.

**Goal, pressure, or ending:**
- The players are looking for multiple coordinates towards and safe route in order to escape to a safe zone.
- If the tank is destroyed, the players immediately lose.

**Hook:**
- "Control a fixed-turret tank with a buddy and traverse an abandoned, yet dangerous battleground for a chance at escape."

**Smallest useful physical prototype:**
- 10x10 Grid on a Board with either fixed event spaces that can be rolled on (like on Monopoly), or tiles could be randomized by being entirely empty and the players roll to see which event they get on the space they land on.
- Would require 3 Dice: A D6 (Normal Six-Sided Die), a D10 (10-Sided Die), and a D100 (10-Sided Die based in 10s).

**Question I want listeners to answer:**
- Should the Gunner only be able to see in a direct line in front of them or a limited cone in front of them?
- How do you think I can handle overwhelming the Driver and boring the Gunner?
- Should the tank have an overall health pool or one for the main body and one for the treads?

Keep the cards as working notes. The workshop does not add a separate graded pitch submission.
