# IT265 Module 2: One-Page Treatment

**Working game title:**
> Cohort

Write one coherent, one-page-length treatment. Use the four prompts to begin, then combine your answers into natural prose.

## Four-Sentence Core

1. **Premise:** Who is the player, what is the situation, and what problem matters?
   The player builds a unit lineup and chooses a small list of equipment that will be randomly drawn from a stack to play against an opponent doing the same.
2. **Experience and loop:** What should play feel like, and what does the player repeatedly decide or do?
   Play should feel methodical and provoke tactical thinking, having the player constantly think about what unit to bring along, what actions to take, and when to use energy.
3. **Goal and pressure:** What changes, what limits the player, and how does play end?
   Being a PvP game, unit health and energy are precious resources that need to be accounted for, with play only ending when the opponent's units are all wiped out.
4. **Scope:** What makes this version distinctive, and what can you test first with affordable physical materials?
   This version is distinctive as I can design a simple field and write up card actions in a few hours, with the actual art and cards taking a while to create, as I discovered that I have a photo printer at home.

## Treatment Draft
Cohort is a competitive PvP deck-builder battler game in which each player fights on a field consisting of 4 Slots/Positions called Ranks and have to build a team of varying rank sizes and power. A player will also have the ability to select up to 5 Equipment/Buff cards that will be randomly shuffled into a deck that can be accessed during gameplay for an energy cost. 

Each turn, the player gains 1 energy point and possibly more depending on the cards they have. The core loop focuses around which units to use, whether or not to conserve an energy point or use it now, and whether to move units, forgoing the ability to use an action for that turn. The objective is the clear the opponent's field by destroying all of their units. Reducing the number of units that an opponent has takes another possible threat off the board.

The first prototype of the game will have the field, energy tokens, at least 2 basic unit cards of varying rank sizes (to allow each player at least one copy of each unit), as well as up to 5 equipment cards, also having at least 2 copies of each.

## Reader Check
Each turn, the player gains 1 energy point and possibly more depending on the cards they have. The core loop focuses around which units to use, whether or not to conserve an energy point or use it now, and whether to move units, forgoing the ability to use an action for that turn. The objective is the clear the opponent's field by destroying all of their units. Reducing the number of units that an opponent has takes another possible threat off the board.

The gameplay loop consists of Phases, in which each player will take a turn and choose an action to do with a unit before the other player goes. Each unit has at minimum 1 action per phase, with bigger units, that take up multiple ranks, may have more than 1 action per phase. Each unit can only act so many times per phase to prevent players from spamming a large powerful unit over and over again every time it is there turn, forcing them to choose the right time when to use a unit's special action.
