## Unique Selling Point (USP)
*What will make your games stand out from others, particularly in the indie/board game space?*

> My game will stand out from others by attempting to emulate game mechanics from some of my favorite games such as the Stress Mechanic from Darkest Dungeon. Another possible USP would be one of the themes that I want to explore: the story following a regular no-name soldier/adventurer/character rather than some developed character. Basically, the character grows with the player.
